Velocity, Dynamics & Swing

Level scales each voice's natural velocity (World's fixed accent, Population A/B's live density, Metronome's fixed tap). Mode adds variation on top: Jitter = independent random wobble per hit. Accent = occasional loud pop at a fixed chance. Walk = a slow, smoothly drifting offset that wanders between hits instead of resetting each time. Feeds both the internal synths and MIDI velocity, so what you hear and what you send out always match. Swing delays each voice's off-beat 16th notes independently — 0% is straight, 100% pushes the off-beat to a full triplet shuffle — so e.g. the hi-hat can swing while the kick stays locked.

World — the food rhythm

Food appears on these 5 steps of every 16-beat cycle. A creature that plays exactly on these steps and nowhere else is the goal.

Challenge

Generation 1 / 12 Best match – Parents none

How to play: click a creature (or press its number key) to make it a parent — the first becomes parent A, the second parent B; clicking selects a fresh pair. Hit Audition selection to hear your two parents against the food rhythm, then Breed next generation: your two parents' genes are crossed over and slightly mutated into two children, which replace the two weakest creatures (your parents are never discarded). Score = how closely the creature's pattern matches the food rhythm over a full shared cycle: food hits counted, missed food and activity without food both penalized. Breed deliberately — you have only a set number of generations.
