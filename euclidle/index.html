What's a Euclidean rhythm?

The Björklund / Euclidean algorithm spaces k onsets (hits) as evenly as possible across n steps — the same math behind the Euclidean algorithm for GCDs, applied to rhythm. Today's secret is always a coprime pair (gcd(k,n) = 1), which guarantees the pattern has a unique period and can't be confused with a shorter repeating pattern — so every sound maps back to exactly one answer.

How to play

Every day, Euclidle picks one secret rhythm — k onsets spread as evenly as possible across n steps (a Euclidean rhythm), always with an audible downbeat on step 0.

Hit Play target to listen, then guess the two numbers k and n. After each guess you get:

• Arrows on k and n telling you to go higher or lower.
• A colored step diff comparing your guess's pattern to the target, step by step.

You have 6 guesses. Re-hear the target and your guess as often as you like — this is a cozy puzzle, not a stingy one. The secret is always coprime (gcd(k,n)=1), so there's exactly one right answer — no rotations to worry about.
